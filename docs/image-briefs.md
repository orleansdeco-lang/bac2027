# Photographic Briefs for SHATER (الشاطر) Baccalaureate Platform

> **Guiding Principle**: Avoid generic American/European stock photography and artificial plastic AI avatars. Every photo must feel authentically Algerian (traditional wooden desks, authentic school notebooks, warm desk lamps, tea/coffee glasses, natural Algerian homes and high school architecture).
