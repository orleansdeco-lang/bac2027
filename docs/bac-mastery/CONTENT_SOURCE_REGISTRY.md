## 1. Classification Framework

Every piece of educational content, curriculum structure, scoring coefficient, and pedagogical recommendation in BAC Mastery must be grounded in an authoritative source. Sources are classified into five mutually exclusive tiers:

| Tier Code | Classification Name | Definition & Evidentiary Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`OFFICIAL_CURRENT`** | Current Official Source | Directly confirmed by an active official ministerial document, decree, or authoritative circular explicitly valid for the current/upcoming academic cycle (BAC 2027). |
| **`OFFICIAL_HISTORICAL`** | Historical Official Source | Issued by the Algerian Ministry of National Education (MEN) or ONEC, but dating from a prior academic year. Validated as historical precedent; flagged for monitoring if new circulars are pending. |
| **`BAC_MASTERY_DERIVED`** | Internal Pedagogical Model | Proprietary pedagogical taxonomy, atomic skill decomposition, error diagnostics, and remediation sequences authored specifically for the BAC Mastery learning engine. |
| **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`** | Peer-Reviewed Cognitive Science | Rigorous scientific research published in peer-reviewed journals (cognitive load theory, retrieval practice, distributed practice, error metacognition, deliberate practice). |
| **`UNVERIFIED`** | Tentative / Unconfirmed | Claims or parameters lacking direct ministerial or empirical evidence for BAC 2027. Must never be presented as official policy. |

### Source SRC-005: Retrieval Practice & Testing Effect
- **Source Identifier**: `SRC-RESEARCH-ROEDIGER-2006`
- **Authors**: Henry L. Roediger III & Jeffrey D. Karpicke
- **Publication**: *Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention*
- **Journal**: Psychological Science, 17(3), 249–255 (2006)
- **DOI**: `10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x`
- **Evidentiary Tier**: Tier 1 (High Empirical Rigor — Randomized Controlled Trials)
- **Core Principle**: Active retrieval of information from memory produces stronger and more durable neural pathways than repeated passive re-reading or highlighted study.
- **BAC Mastery Implementation**: Immediate active recall prompts at the end of every lesson, practice-before-theory diagnostic routines, and closed-book retrieval gates.
- **Classification**: **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**

### Source SRC-006: Comprehensive Analysis of Learning Techniques
- **Source Identifier**: `SRC-RESEARCH-DUNLOSKY-2013`
- **Authors**: John Dunlosky, Katherine A. Rawson, Elizabeth J. Marsh, Mitchell J. Nathan, & Daniel T. Willingham
- **Publication**: *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology*
- **Journal**: Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58 (2013)
- **DOI**: `10.1177/1529100612453266`
- **Evidentiary Tier**: Tier 1 (Comprehensive Meta-Analysis & Systematic Review)
- **Core Principle**: Practice testing (retrieval practice) and distributed practice across time exhibit high utility across all educational domains; summarization, highlighting, and passive rereading exhibit low utility.
- **BAC Mastery Implementation**: Spaced repetition intervals in roadmaps, mini-checkpoint scheduling, rejection of passive highlighting tools.
- **Classification**: **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**

### Source SRC-007: Interleaving Effects in Mathematics Education
- **Source Identifier**: `SRC-RESEARCH-ROHRER-2007`
- **Authors**: Doug Rohrer & Kelli Taylor
- **Publication**: *The Shuffling of Mathematics Problems Improves Learning*
- **Journal**: Instructional Science, 35(6), 481–498 (2007)
- **DOI**: `10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8`
- **Evidentiary Tier**: Tier 1 (Empirical Educational Trials)
- **Core Principle**: Interleaving different types of problems forces students to actively select the correct mathematical strategy rather than blindly applying the single formula practiced in a blocked session.
- **BAC Mastery Implementation**: Mixed mini-exams and multi-topic weakness checks that combine analysis, geometry, and probability in a single assessment session.
- **Classification**: **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**

### Source SRC-008: Cognitive Load Theory & Worked Examples
- **Source Identifier**: `SRC-RESEARCH-SWELLER-1988`
- **Author**: John Sweller
- **Publication**: *Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning*
- **Journal**: Cognitive Science, 12(2), 257–285 (1988)
- **DOI**: `10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4`
- **Evidentiary Tier**: Tier 1 (Foundational Cognitive Science)
- **Core Principle**: Novices learn significantly more efficiently from studying well-structured worked examples than from unguided problem solving, which overburdens working memory.
- **BAC Mastery Implementation**: Dedicated 3-element worked examples in every lesson (*Example -> Thinking Process -> Step-by-Step Formal Execution*) before practice questions.
- **Classification**: **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**

### Source SRC-009: Metacognition & Hypercorrection of High-Confidence Errors
- **Source Identifier**: `SRC-RESEARCH-METCALFE-2017`
- **Author**: Janet Metcalfe
- **Publication**: *Learning From Errors*
- **Journal**: Annual Review of Psychology, 68, 465–489 (2017)
- **DOI**: `10.1146/annurev-psych-010416-044022`
- **Evidentiary Tier**: Tier 1 (Systematic Cognitive Review)
- **Core Principle**: Errors made with high confidence produce a disproportionately large attentional spike upon immediate correction, making error recovery one of the most potent drivers of conceptual change.
- **BAC Mastery Implementation**: Error Lab architecture, immediate feedback on distractor choices, and mandatory 5-15 minute targeted repair guides.
- **Classification**: **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**

## 3. Source Registry Maintenance Protocol
1. **Periodic Review**: The source registry must be re-inspected whenever the Algerian Ministry of National Education issues new decrees in the official bulletin (*النشرة الرسمية للتربية الوطنية*).
2. **Deprecation Handling**: If a decree supersedes an existing historical source (e.g. any update to Arrêté n° 54/2007), the old entry is retained with status `SUPERSEDED` and the new decree registered as `OFFICIAL_CURRENT`.
3. **Zero Fabricated Citations**: In accordance with the Anti-Hallucination Constitution, all academic citations, dates, ISBNs, and quotes must be verifiably authentic.
