## Content Defect Classification Guidelines
Whenever a student points out an ambiguity, phrasing error, or calculation discrepancy, log it immediately before making any edits:

- **Severity Critical (P1)**: Mathematically or scientifically incorrect answer key; broken formula notation rendering question unsolvable.
- **Severity Medium (P2)**: Ambiguous prompt phrasing where two options could be defended; confusing distractors.
- **Severity Low (P3)**: Minor grammatical or typographical artifact; slightly awkward RTL layout.
