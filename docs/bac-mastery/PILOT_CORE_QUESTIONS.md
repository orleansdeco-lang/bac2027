# BAC Mastery — Pilot Core Questions Evaluation Rubric
**Controlled Real-Student Validation (Prompt 18.2 § 8 & 9)**

---

## The 4 Fundamental Student Questions
The core hypothesis of BAC Mastery is that a student who uses the system can answer these 4 questions at any moment without external guidance:

1. **السؤال 1: أنا وين؟ (Where am I?)**
   - *Expected Evidence*: Current overall progress, diagnostic baseline signal, demonstrated skill count, current level in core subjects.
2. **السؤال 2: واش ناقصني؟ (What is missing / what is my gap?)**
   - *Expected Evidence*: Specific bottleneck identified by diagnostic or Error Lab, weak domains, recurring error types.
3. **السؤال 3: واش ندير دروك؟ (What do I do right now?)**
   - *Expected Evidence*: Exactly ONE clear next best action highlighted on Dashboard/Roadmap without choice overload.
4. **السؤال 4: كيفاش نعرف بلي تعلمتها فعلاً؟ (How do I know I actually learned it?)**
   - *Expected Evidence*: Retrieval practice evidence, targeted error repair, passing an independent isomorphic retest twin.

---

## Evaluation Scoring Criteria

- **`INDEPENDENT` (مستقل - 2 pts)**: The student answers clearly within 10 seconds of viewing their screen, pointing directly to the supporting UI element.
- **`HINT_ASSISTED` (بمساعدة خفيفة - 1 pt)**: The student hesitates or expresses confusion, but answers correctly after a neutral clarification (e.g. *"واش راك تشوف في أعلى الشاشة؟"*).
- **`UNABLE` (غير قادر - 0 pts)**: The student cannot answer, points to unrelated elements, or expresses complete disorientation.

## Benchmark Thresholds for Green Pilot Signal
- **Q3 ("واش ندير دروك؟")**: Must achieve $\ge 80\%$ `INDEPENDENT` identification across the cohort. If $< 60\%$, the UI suffers from `CHOICE_OVERLOAD` or unclear call-to-action.
- **Q4 ("كيفاش نعرف بلي تعلمتها؟")**: Must achieve $\ge 70\%$ understanding that mastery requires passing the retest twin, rather than merely clicking "Next".
