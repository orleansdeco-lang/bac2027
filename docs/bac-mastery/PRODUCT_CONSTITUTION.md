# BAC MASTERY — PRODUCT CONSTITUTION

> **"ماشي واش تقرا. كيفاش توصل."**
> *(It's not just what you study. It's how you reach your goal.)*
> From current level → target → roadmap → execution → mastery → exam readiness.

---

## 1. Core Mission

BAC Mastery is a digital learning system engineered exclusively for Algerian BAC students. 

The product is **NOT** simply a repository of summaries, downloadable PDFs, exercise compilations, or an automated AI chatbot.

Its singular mission is:
> **Help a student move from their current academic level to their target BAC result through a clear, adaptive roadmap — while protecting their mental energy, rest, and future orientation.**

---

## 2. The 4 Balanced Pillars

BAC Mastery rejects the toxic, one-dimensional study culture that tells students only: *"Study more."*

True exam excellence is built on four interdependent pillars:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      THE 4 PILLARS                          │
├───────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┤
│   1. STUDY    │   2. PROGRESS   │   3. MIND   │  4. FUTURE  │
│ Methodology,  │ Clear metrics,  │ Wellbeing,  │ Strategic   │
│ Active recall,│ Bottlenecks,    │ Rest, Calm, │ Orientations│
│ Deep practice │ Error repair    │ Anti-burnout│ Life after  │
│ & Retention   │ & Milestones    │ & Momentum  │ the BAC     │
└───────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┤
```

### Pillar 1: STUDY
- Not passive reading or mindless highlighting.
- Focuses on cognitive depth: **Understand → Close → Recall → Apply → Test**.
- Stream-specific methodologies (e.g., experimental scientific reasoning, mathematical problem modeling, literary synthesis).

### Pillar 2: PROGRESS
- Transparent feedback on actual level vs. target level.
- Identification of the single highest-leverage **bottleneck** at any given moment.
- Relentless focus on **Error Analysis** and **Repair Missions** rather than continuous unfocused practice.

### Pillar 3: MIND
- Study wellbeing layer: monitoring daily energy states (Good, Normal, Tired, Stressed).
- **Rest is part of the roadmap**, never an unearned luxury or a reward.
- Zero guilt, zero shaming: intelligent **Recovery Mode** when a student falls behind.

### Pillar 4: FUTURE
- The BAC is an academic gateway, not the destination of a student's life.
- Helps students contextualize their target score with desired university faculties, schools (e.g., ESI, ENS, Medicine, Architecture, Polytech), and vocational aspirations.

## 3. The Central Questions

BAC Mastery is designed to answer the questions that students actually struggle with daily:

1. **Where am I now?** (Precise, realistic current estimated level)
2. **Where do I want to go?** (Target BAC score and future specialization)
3. **What is preventing me from getting there?** (Active academic bottlenecks)
4. **What should I do today?** (Bite-sized, executable daily missions)
5. **How do I know that I improved?** (Demonstrated mastery, not study hours)
6. **Why did I make mistakes?** (Systematic error taxonomy)
7. **What should I repair?** (Targeted remediation exercises)
8. **When should I review?** (Algorithmic spaced repetition)
9. **When should I rest?** (Mandatory downtime and cognitive reset)
10. **Am I progressing toward my target?** (Visual gap reduction)
11. **What happens if I fall behind?** (Adaptive recovery plan without backlog paralysis)
12. **What future am I preparing for?** (Connecting effort to future goals)

## 5. Constitutional Non-Negotiables & Red Lines

To preserve focus and student respect, BAC Mastery strictly enforces the following boundaries:

1. **No SaaS Bloat**: No endless menus, complex workflows, or confusing settings.
2. **No Toxic Gamification**: No casino-like animations, streak anxiety, or competitive leaderboards that induce panic.
3. **No Direct AI API in V1**: AI integration is provided via an exportable **Student Intelligence Report** (prompt bridge) that students can copy into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude.
4. **No Payments or Gateways in V1**: Focused on validating student transformation with 0 DZD infrastructure cost.
5. **No Marketplace / Teacher Hiring**: The focus is student self-efficacy and structured execution.
6. **Mobile-First**: Optimized for smartphone screens, where Algerian students actually study and revise.
7. **Bilingual & Culturally Grounded**: First-class Arabic and French support, designed around Algerian secondary education standards.
