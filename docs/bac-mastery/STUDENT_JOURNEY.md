# BAC MASTERY — STUDENT JOURNEY

## 1. Journey Overview

The BAC Mastery experience is structured as an intentional academic expedition. It replaces chaotic last-minute cramming and unfocused study groups with a calm, deliberate, step-by-step transformation.

```
PHASE 1: DISCOVERY & ALIGNMENT
Landing Page ──> Method Discovery ──> Start Onboarding

PHASE 2: PROFILE & INTENT (The Strategic Vector)
Select Stream ──> Target Score ──> Study Hours ──> Future Vision ──> Perceived Weaknesses

PHASE 3: DIAGNOSTIC & GAP DISCOVERY
Initial Diagnostic ──> Baseline Assessment ──> Gap Analysis ──> Bottleneck Identification

PHASE 4: THE DAILY EXECUTION CYCLE
Personalized Roadmap ──> Daily Missions ──> Study ──> Practice ──> Error Lab ──> Repair ──> Mastery

PHASE 5: LONG-TERM CALIBRATION & BAC READINESS
Weekly Review ──> Adaptive Recalibration ──> Exam Mode Transition ──> Exam Day Readiness
```

---

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Journey Breakdown

### Phase 1: Discovery & Alignment
1. **Landing Page**: Student arrives and encounters the core truth: *"ماشي واش تقرا. كيفاش توصل."* (It's not just what you study, but how you get there).
2. **Method Discovery**: The student learns that BAC success is an engineering problem (diagnostic, gap analysis, targeted repair, recovery), not a contest of brute memorization.
3. **Commitment**: The student opts in to follow a structured roadmap rather than endless, unfocused task lists.

### Phase 2: Strategic Onboarding & Initial Gap Mapping (Phase 2 Implemented)
1. **Welcome & Vision**: Grounded in *"ماشي واش تقرا. كيفاش توصل."*.
2. **Education Level**: Selected BAC, with an architecture designed for future BEM.
3. **Stream & Specialty**: Selects 1 of 6 BAC streams; for Technique Math, selects engineering branch (Génie Civil, Mécanique, Électrique, Procédés) assigning coefficient 7.
4. **Target Score**: Defines ambitious yet clear target (10.0 to 20.0).
5. **Level Estimation ("مستواك الحالي — تقديرك")**:
   - Explicitly distinguished from the future empirical diagnostic.
   - Evaluates subjects on a 1-5 scale to establish an initial weighted baseline grade.
6. **Available Study Time**: Calibrates realistic hours/week outside high school.
7. **Future Objective**: Anchors effort to specific faculties or aspirations.
8. **Obstacles & Bottleneck Discovery**: Captures root blockers (application methodology, consistency, time, active recall, backlog).
9. **Study Energy & State**: Checks daily wellbeing (Good, Normal, Tired, Stressed).
10. **Summary & Verification**: Reviews choices before generating the profile.
11. **The Strategic Map (`/roadmap`)**:
   - The first major milestone: shows Target, Estimated Baseline, Approximate Gap, Primary Bottleneck, and First Mission Preview.
   - Prepares the student for the upcoming empirical diagnostic (`/diagnostic`).

### Phase 3: Diagnostic & Gap Analysis
1. **Multi-Faceted Diagnostic**:
   - Diagnostic tests are not standard school exams; they isolate *Knowledge*, *Reasoning*, and *Methodology*.
2. **Baseline Calibration**: Current estimated score calculated across all coefficient-weighted subjects.
3. **Gap Visualization**:
   - Total Gap: e.g., Current 11.80 → Target 16.50 (Gap: +4.70 points).
   - **Primary Bottleneck Revealed**: The single subject/domain with the highest leverage to close the gap.
   - Example: Science has coefficient 6 in Sciences Expérimentales; a 4-point lift in Science yields +1.20 to the overall BAC score.

### Phase 4: Daily Execution Cycle (The Engine in Action)
1. **Mind & Energy Check**: Quick 1-click check-in (Good, Normal, Tired, Stressed). The day's workload adapts immediately.
2. **Daily Missions Briefing**:
   - The student is presented with 2–4 targeted missions totaling the day's budgeted time (e.g., 60–90 min).
   - *Mission 1 (30 min)*: Core understanding of complex numbers (Methodology & worked example).
   - *Mission 2 (25 min)*: 3 targeted BAC exercises under timed conditions.
   - *Mission 3 (15 min)*: Error Lab review of yesterday's calculation traps.
3. **Study & Practice Execution**: Active recall, guided solving, and self-testing.
4. **Error Lab Logging**:
   - For every incorrect answer, the student logs the failure reason using the 10-type error taxonomy.
5. **Targeted Repair**: The system generates a focused mini-repair task for recurring error patterns.
6. **Mastery Validation**: A brief re-test validates that the error has been neutralized.

### Phase 5: Weekly Calibration & Exam Transition
1. **Weekly Review**: Every 7 days, the student reviews:
   - Missions completed vs. planned.
   - Errors corrected and converted into mastery.
   - Energy patterns (Mind check summary).
2. **Adaptive Recalibration**: If a student is ahead or behind, the roadmap dynamically recalibrates without accumulating guilt-inducing backlogs.
3. **Exam Mode (Final 60-90 Days)**:
   - Shifts from learning to **Simulation & Strategy**: 3-4 hour full BAC trial papers, pacing calibration, sleep optimization, and final confidence consolidation.
