# BAC Mastery — Pilot Qualitative Observation Sheet
**Controlled Real-Student Validation (Prompt 18.2 § 26)**

---

## Instructions for the Field Observer
- Maintain factual objectivity. Record what the student **did** and **said**, not what you assume they thought.
- Note exact timestamps and screen locations whenever friction occurs.
- Never write the participant's real name; use pseudonyms `STUDENT-01` through `STUDENT-10`.

---

## Observation Template

```markdown
### Participant Session Record
- **Participant ID**: STUDENT-XX
- **Session Number**: 1 (First Session) / 2 (Next-Day Return) / 3 (Follow-up)
- **Device & Browser**: (e.g. Samsung Galaxy A52 / Chrome Mobile 122 or Laptop / Firefox)
- **Session Duration**: XX minutes
- **Observer Name**: [Observer Initials]

#### Key Behavioral Milestones
1. **First Confusion**: (Screen name, exact button or text where the student paused or asked a question)
2. **First Moment of Value**: (Moment where student expressed relief, said "فهمت", or smiled)
3. **First Abandonment Risk**: (Moment where student seemed frustrated, stuck, or looked away from screen)
4. **First Successful Independent Action**: (First action completed completely without hesitation)
5. **First Error**: (Which question ID? What was the student's reaction?)
6. **First Successful Repair**: (Did they read the repair guide? Did they understand what changed?)
7. **First Successful Retest**: (Did they solve the isomorphic twin independently?)
8. **First Demonstrated Mastery**: (Did they notice the green mastery badge? How did they interpret it?)
```
