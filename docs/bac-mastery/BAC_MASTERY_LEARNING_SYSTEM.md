## 1. Executive Vision & Educational Purpose

BAC Mastery is engineered from a single educational premise:

> **"من مستواك الحالي إلى هدفك"**  
> **"ماشي واش تقرا. كيفاش توصل."**  
> *(It is not merely what you study. It is how you reach your goal.)*

The Algerian Baccalauréat (BAC) is a high-stakes, competitive national exam. Traditional preparation frequently traps students in passive, high-exhaustion, low-retention routines:
- Stacking PDF summaries without active recall.
- Watching hours of YouTube solution videos passively (creating the *fluency bias* / illusion of understanding).
- Mass-solving identical exercises without learning how to select methods (*blocked practice* failure).
- Abandoning revision after a single correct answer.
- Experiencing burnout, sleep deprivation, and panic when falling behind a rigid schedule.

BAC Mastery is **NOT**:
- A PDF or summary repository.
- An exercise bank or past-paper collection.
- A rigid calendar ordering students to "study 10 hours a day."
- A flashcard-only app or simple gamified quiz.
- A generic AI chatbot producing speculative text.

BAC Mastery **IS**:
A **Learning Operating System (LOS)** that continuously converts empirical student evidence into the **single next best learning action**, answering at every moment:
1. **أين أنا؟** *(What is my calibrated current level?)*
2. **واش ناقصني؟** *(What exact gap or bottleneck is holding me back?)*
3. **واش ندير دروك؟** *(What is my single next priority action right now?)*
4. **علاش هذي هي المهمة؟** *(Why is this specific mission prioritized?)*
5. **كيفاش نعرف بلي تعلمتها؟** *(What observable evidence proves I mastered it?)*
6. **متى نرجع لها؟** *(When should this concept be retrieved to prevent forgetting?)*
7. **ماذا نفعل إذا فشلت؟** *(What targeted micro-repair will fix the root misconception?)*
8. **كيف ننتقل من التدريب إلى مستوى BAC الحقيقي؟** *(How do we scaffold from guided practice to exam-level transfer?)*

## 3. The Study Engine (Universal 13-Element Lesson Architecture)

Reading or watching an explanation is **exposure**, never proof of mastery. The Study Engine operationalizes lesson engagement through 13 sequential elements:

1. **المعاينة والأفق (Preview & Target Horizon)**: State the exact BAC question this lesson solves.
2. **تنشيط المكتسبات القبلية (Activate Prerequisites)**: Rapid 1-minute retrieval check of prerequisite definitions from 1AS/2AS.
3. **الفهم المفاهيمي العميق (Conceptual Core)**: Deconstruct the mechanism, definition, or physical law without unnecessary jargon.
4. **الشرح البسيط باللغة الطبيعية (Intuitive Explanation)**: Explain the idea in dignified, accessible Arabic (or subject language).
5. **المثال المحلول النموذجي (Annotated Worked Example)**: Provide step-by-step reasoning demonstrating *why* each step was chosen.
6. **نموذج التفكير الذهني (Mental Modeling)**: "كيف نفكر في هذا السؤال؟" Unpack the internal monologue of an expert solving this problem.
7. **إغلاق المصدر (Close the Source)**: Mandatory prompt instructing the student to cover the explanation.
8. **الاسترجاع النشط الفوري (Active Recall Check)**: Prompt the student to restate the law, condition, or formula from memory.
9. **التطبيق الموجه (Guided Application)**: First scaffolded exercise with intermediate sanity hints.
10. **التطبيق المستقل (Independent Practice)**: Unassisted problem solving to generate genuine performance data.
11. **التغذية الراجعة التشخيصية (Diagnostic Feedback)**: Immediate explanation separating the correct path from common misconceptions.
12. **مخبر الأخطاء والترميم (Error Diagnosis & Repair)**: Seamless handoff if an error is made.
13. **الربط بنصوص البكالوريا (BAC Exam Transfer)**: Explicit citation of past BAC exam sessions where this competence appeared.

### Epistemic Progression Spectrum
The system rigorously separates student engagement levels:
$$\text{EXPOSURE} \longrightarrow \text{UNDERSTANDING SIGNAL} \longrightarrow \text{RETRIEVAL} \longrightarrow \text{APPLICATION} \longrightarrow \text{TRANSFER} \longrightarrow \text{MASTERY}$$
Under no circumstances are these collapsed into a superficial "percentage completed" metric.

## 4. The Memory Engine: Encoding to Transfer

Memory durability is governed by the cognitive pathway:
$$\text{ENCODE} \longrightarrow \text{RETRIEVE} \longrightarrow \text{SPACE} \longrightarrow \text{INTERLEAVE} \longrightarrow \text{REAPPLY}$$

### Cognitive Levels Distinguished:
- **Recognition (التعرف السطحي)**: Feeling familiar with a formula when looking at it. (Dangerously misleading; fosters false confidence).
- **Recall (الاسترجاع البسيط)**: Reproducing a definition or statement without notes.
- **Active Recall (الاسترجاع النشط الموجه)**: Generating the condition, proof, or relationship under challenge.
- **Application (التطبيق الإجرائي)**: Executing the steps of a known procedure correctly.
- **Transfer (التحويل والتكيف)**: Recognizing that a novel, unfamiliar BAC problem requires this specific concept and adapting the procedure accordingly.

> [!NOTE]
> BAC Mastery makes **zero unsupported quantitative claims** such as "active recall improves grades by 80%." Active recall is employed because empirical cognitive psychology demonstrates it creates superior retrieval pathways compared to passive rereading.

## 5. The Spaced Review Engine (Adaptive Evidence-Based Scheduling)

### 5.1 Rejection of Rigid Fixed Schedules
BAC Mastery explicitly rejects hardcoding a rigid interval law (e.g. Day 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30) as an immutable truth. Such fixed schedules ignore individual student performance, difficulty, and lapse history.

### 5.2 Multi-Factor Adaptive Interval Model
Review intervals are calculated dynamically using six empirical evidence vectors:
1. **Correctness ($c \in \{0, 1\}$)**: Incorrect retrieval immediately collapses interval to 1.0 day and triggers a repair cycle.
2. **Student Confidence ($\kappa \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$)**: Low confidence ($\kappa \le 2$) indicates fragile memory and yields a conservative growth factor ($1.2\times$). High confidence ($\kappa \ge 4$) yields robust expansion ($2.2\times$).
3. **Response Speed Ratio ($\tau = t_{\text{actual}} / t_{\text{expected}}$)**:
   - $\tau > 2.0$ (High cognitive struggle) $\implies$ growth factor discounted by $0.8\times$.
   - $\tau < 0.6 \wedge \kappa \ge 4$ (Effortless fluency) $\implies$ growth factor boosted by $1.15\times$.
4. **Lapse History ($L$)**: Chronic past lapses penalize interval expansion: $\text{penalty} = \max(0.6, 1.0 - 0.1 \times L)$.
5. **Decay Factor ($\delta$)**: Stabilizes with consecutive successes, accelerates under recurring errors.
6. **Days Elapsed**: Determines whether a skill is `fresh`, `due`, `overdue`, or `critical`.

### 5.3 Review Urgency Matrix

| Status | Condition | Priority Queue Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`fresh`** | Current Date $<$ Next Review Due | Retain in maintenance queue; do not interrupt active missions |
| **`due`** | Current Date $\ge$ Next Review Due (Day 0) | Eligible for daily secondary review slot |
| **`overdue`** | Next Review Due past by 1–3 days | Elevated to high-priority review slot |
| **`critical`** | Next Review Due past by $> 3$ days | Elevated above new lessons; risk of memory trace extinction |

## 6. The Practice & Interleaving Engines

### 6.1 Progressive Practice Hierarchy
Practice items progress systematically through 6 pedagogical tiers:
1. **Guided Practice (تطبيق موجه)**: Single concept, hints visible, step-by-step constraints.
2. **Semi-Guided Practice (تطبيق شبه موجه)**: Distractors present, hints on demand, focus on execution.
3. **Independent Practice (تطبيق مستقل)**: Zero hints, strict timer, realistic distractor options.
4. **Mixed Practice (تمارين مختلطة)**: Combining 2 or more related skills from the same unit.
5. **Transfer Practice (تطبيق تحويلي)**: Applying the concept in a non-standard or cross-unit context.
6. **Exam Level (مستوى البكالوريا)**: Authentic or adapted BAC problem with official grading structure.

### 6.2 Blocked vs. Interleaved Practice
- **Blocked Practice ($AAAA, BBBB, CCCC$)**: Used exclusively during initial procedural acquisition (e.g. learning how to compute a derivative via chain rule for the first time).
- **Interleaved Practice ($ABCA, BCAB, CABA$)**: Introduced once baseline competence is demonstrated. Forces the student to solve the primary BAC cognitive challenge: **"Which method belongs to this problem?"**

### 6.3 Interleaving Rules
Interleaving is never random. Pedagogically sound interleaving requires:
- Same subject or strictly connected interdisciplinary skills.
- Prerequisite compatibility (unmastered skills are never interleaved into complex problems).
- Comparable difficulty tiers (difficulty difference $\le 1$).

## 7. Error Intelligence, Repair & Retest Engine

### 7.1 Comprehensive Error Taxonomy
Mistakes are classified into 10 structured cognitive and methodological types:
1. `forgot_information`: Forgot the definition, rule, constant, or theorem.
2. `misunderstood_concept`: Fundamental misconception of the underlying phenomenon or mechanism.
3. `methodology_error`: Knew the idea but failed official Algerian BAC answer structuring.
4. `calculation_error`: Sign mistake, algebraic slip, or arithmetic error.
5. `misread_question`: Missed initial conditions, units, or question constraints.
6. `rushed`: Responded prematurely without verifying alternatives.
7. `lack_of_practice`: Recognized the concept but lacked procedural fluency.
8. `time_management`: Ran out of time on a timed task.
9. `attention_error`: Slipped on an obvious element due to fatigue or distraction.
10. `unknown`: Unattributed ambiguity; triggers step-by-step diagnostic breakdown.

### 7.2 Recurring Error Escalation Rule
$$\text{Count}\big(e \in \text{Errors} \;\big|\; e.\text{skillId} = \text{skillId} \;\wedge\; e.\text{errorType} = \text{errorType}\big) \ge 2 \implies \text{isRecurring} = \text{true}$$
- Recurring errors immediately halt advancement to new skills in that subject.
- The system prioritizes root-cause repair over mindless repetition: *"تكرر نفس نوع الخطأ. الأفضل نصلح السبب بدل ما نزيدو تمارين."*

### 7.3 The Targeted Repair Protocol (5–15 Minutes Max)
Repair is never "go reread the entire chapter." It is an operational surgical intervention:
- Identify the exact line of breakdown.
- Review a focused micro-example illustrating the misconception.
- Execute a 1-question active micro-drill.
- Transition immediately to the Isomorphic Retest Twin.

### 7.4 The Isomorphic Retest Twin & 2-Cycle Failure Limit
- **Isomorphic Retest Twin**: Tests the identical competence with altered numbers, inverted variables, or different algebraic functions. It is never a simple repetition of the exact failed question.
- **Max 2-Cycle Policy**:
  - Cycle 1 Failure $\implies$ Re-engage repair steps with alternative framing.
  - Cycle 2 Failure $\implies$ Transition skill status to `needs_more_work` ("تحتاج هذه النقطة إلى عمل إضافي"). The mission is paused and scheduled for delayed recovery to prevent cognitive burnout and frustration paralysis.

## 8. The Evidence-Based Mastery Engine

### 8.1 Mastery States
- **`not_yet` (لم نثبتها بعد)**: Unaddressed, unassessed, or failed 2 retests requiring deeper recovery.
- **`emerging` (في طور التحسن)**: Initial practice answered correctly, but unverified by a twin retest or delayed review.
- **`demonstrated` (تم إثبات التحكم ✓)**: Error identified $\to$ repair completed $\to$ unseen twin retest passed with high confidence.

### 8.2 Non-Permanence of Mastery (`review_due`)
Mastery is an empirical state, not a permanent trophy. When the Spaced Review Engine calculates that retention risk has decayed past the critical threshold, the skill transitions to:
$$\text{demonstrated} \longrightarrow \text{review\_due}$$
The student must successfully complete a retrieval challenge to renew their demonstrated mastery status.

## 9. Subject Methodology Families

BAC Mastery rejects forcing every subject into an identical STEM procedure. The platform defines **9 distinct methodology families**:

```
                                  SUBJECT METHODOLOGY FAMILIES
                                                │
       ┌──────────────────┬─────────────────────┼────────────────────┬─────────────────┐
       │                  │                     │                    │                 │
  MATHEMATICS      PHYSICS-CHEMISTRY    NATURAL SCIENCES     PHILOSOPHY        LANGUAGES
  Definition       Phenomenon           Document Analysis    Problematic       Comprehension
  Method           Model / Law          Interpretation       Positions         Vocabulary
  Worked Ex.       Equation / Units     Hypothesis           Arguments         Grammar
  Proof / Solve    Application          Conclusion           Writing Method    Production
  Interpretation   Exam Transfer        Scientific Text      Synthesis         Exam Format
```

### 1. Mathematics (الرياضيات)
- **Core Loop**: Definition & Conditions $\to$ Method Selection $\to$ Worked Example $\to$ Guided $\to$ Independent $\to$ Interleaved $\to$ BAC Exam Transfer.
- **Key Focus**: Rigor in mathematical proofs (Intermediate Value Theorem, mathematical induction), category discrimination in unclassified problems.

### 2. Physics & Chemistry (العلوم الفيزيائية)
- **Core Loop**: Phenomenon $\to$ Model $\to$ Law $\to$ Variables $\to$ SI Units $\to$ Conditions $\to$ Literal Equation $\to$ Numerical Calculation $\to$ Physical Sanity Check.
- **Key Focus**: Distinguishing unit conversions ($cm \to m$, $min \to s$), reference frame justification, experimental curve exploitation (slopes, half-life, equivalence points).

### 3. Natural Sciences (علوم الطبيعة والحياة)
- **Core Loop**: Biological Problem $\to$ Document Exploitation (Analysis) $\to$ Interpretation (Causal Link) $\to$ Partial Deduction $\to$ Structured Scientific Text / Schema Synthesis.
- **Key Focus**: Strictly enforcing the official Algerian BAC marking method: separating direct observation from causal interpretation, structured scientific essays with introduction, development, and conclusion.

### 4. History & Geography (التاريخ والجغرافيا)
- **Core Loop**: Historical / Geographic Context $\to$ Structural Timelines / Causality Maps $\to$ Active Retrieval of Terms, Figures & Dates $\to$ Document / Map Exploitation $\to$ Essay Methodology.
- **Key Focus**: Cause-and-effect reasoning over blind textual memorization, precise map localization.

### 5. Philosophy (الفلسفة)
- **Core Loop**: Concept Definition $\to$ Central Problematic $\to$ Thesis Analysis & Evidence $\to$ Antithesis & Critique $\to$ Synthesis / Transcendence $\to$ Formal Essay Writing.
- **Key Focus**: Strict mastery of the 4 Algerian BAC essay methodologies (Dialectic, Comparative, Problem Solving / Investigation, Text Analysis). Zero encouragement of rote essay memorization.

### 6. Islamic Studies (العلوم الإسلامية)
- **Core Loop**: Meaning of Sacred Text (Quran / Hadith) $\to$ Conceptual Definitions $\to$ Doctrinal / Ethical Analysis $\to$ Practical Deductions & Rulings $\to$ Spaced Retrieval of Evidence.
- **Key Focus**: Separating memorization of scriptural evidence from the analytical deduction of legal and ethical rulings.

### 7. Languages (Arabic, French, English, Foreign Languages)
- **Core Principle**: Educational content language matches the target language. UI language is completely decoupled.
- **Core Loop**: Text Comprehension $\to$ Communicative Intent $\to$ Syntax & Lexical Analysis $\to$ Contextual Grammar $\to$ Structured Written Production (e.g. Compte-rendu objectif in French).

### 8. Economics, Management & Accounting (التسيير المحاسبي والمالي والاقتصاد)
- **Core Loop**: Accounting Principles (SCF) $\to$ Transaction Analysis $\to$ Year-End Adjustments (Depreciations, Provisions) $\to$ Financial Statements & Functional Balance Sheets $\to$ Audit & Balance Verification.
- **Key Focus**: Computational accuracy, balance checks between Assets and Liabilities, analytical exploitation of income statements.

### 9. Technique Math Specialties (الهندسة المدنية، الميكانيكية، الكهربائية، الطرائق)
- **Core Principle**: Strictly separate engineering disciplines. An unspecified Technique Math student is **never** defaulted to a single specialty.
- **Specialties**:
  - *Civil Engineering (هندسة مدنية)*: Statics, beams, shear/moment diagrams, reinforced concrete, technical drafting.
  - *Mechanical Engineering (هندسة ميكانيكية)*: Kinematics, resistance of materials (RDM), functional tolerancing, assembly drawings.
  - *Electrical Engineering (هندسة كهربائية)*: Combinational/sequential logic, GRAFCET modeling, electric motors, microcontrollers.
  - *Process Engineering (هندسة الطرائق)*: Organic reaction mechanisms, chemical thermodynamics, unit operations, industrial distillation.

## 10. Planning Engines: Daily & Weekly

### 10.1 Daily Planning Engine: "واش ندير دروك؟"
The student must never open BAC Mastery to an overwhelming wall of 30 tasks. The planner executes a deterministic decision tree producing **ONE PRIMARY MISSION**:

```
Question 1: Does the student have an active unclosed loop (repair_needed or retest_ready)?
  -> YES: Prioritize immediate Retest or Repair.

Question 2: Does the student have an unmastered recurring error?
  -> YES: Prioritize root-cause diagnostic repair.

Question 3: Does the student have a critical or overdue spaced review?
  -> YES: Prioritize 10-minute retrieval check.

Question 4: Does the student have an unresolved diagnostic bottleneck?
  -> YES: Prioritize the foundational skill in the weakest dimension.

Question 5: If all preceding are clear:
  -> Advance to the next sequential curriculum skill in the active topic.
```

### 10.2 Weekly Planning & Adaptation
Every Sunday or weekly review checkpoint, the system audits:
- Net mastered skills vs. emerging skills.
- Persistent error patterns that repeated across topics.
- Overdue review backlog.
- Actual study time completed vs. planned study time.
- Subject balance (flagging neglected high-coefficient subjects).
- Output: An adaptive recalibration adjusting subject time allocation for the upcoming week.

## 11. Energy, Mind & Rest Architecture

### 11.1 Non-Clinical Energy Calibration
Students check in daily with a simple, non-clinical energy indicator:
- **`good` (طاقة عالية)**: Assigns complex problem solving, new abstract concepts, or multi-step practice.
- **`normal` (طبيعية)**: Assigns standard sequential missions.
- **`tired` (متعبة / إرهاق)**: Scales back cognitive load; assigns short active retrieval or targeted micro-repair (15–20 min max).
- **`stressed` (ضغط / توتر)**: Assigns a calm cognitive reset, one achievable micro-mission, and prioritizes rest.

### 11.2 The Non-Punitive Recovery Mode
If a student misses 3 days or 2 weeks of study:
- **NEVER**: "You failed your streak! You have 45 overdue tasks!"
- **ALWAYS**: "Welcome back. Progress is never lost. Let's not worry about the past; here is your single most important mission to restart momentum today."

### 11.3 Rest is Part of the Roadmap
Rest is not an unearned reward; it is an active cognitive phase where memory consolidation occurs. The system explicitly schedules downtime, micro-breaks, and sleep protection.

## 12. Exam Preparation Engine: Stages to BAC Day

The academic year progresses across 5 distinct phases:
1. **Foundation & Coverage (بناء المفاهيم وتغطية المنهاج)**: Focus on deep understanding, worked examples, and procedural competence.
2. **Skill Consolidation & Retesting (تثبيت المهارات ومعالجة الثغرات)**: Elimination of recurring errors, building demonstrated mastery across core topics.
3. **Interleaving & Strategy Selection (الممارسة المختلطة واختيار الطرائق)**: Mixed problem sets without categorical headers.
4. **Timed BAC Simulations (المحاكاة وظروف الامتحان الحقيقية)**: Solving complete 3.5 to 4.5 hour past BAC papers under strict time and presentation conditions.
5. **Final Readiness & Mindset (الاستعداد النهائي والثبات الذهني)**: Targeted high-yield formula retrieval, sleep prioritization, and mental calm.

## 14. Content Language Architecture

The platform strictly enforces the architectural separation between UI localization and educational content language:
- **UI Language**: Governed by student preference (Arabic / French) with full RTL/LTR layout flipping.
- **Educational Content Language**: Governed by the discipline being taught:
