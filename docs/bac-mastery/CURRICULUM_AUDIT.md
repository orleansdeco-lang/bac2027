## 1. Audit Framework & Source Standards

This document presents an authoritative audit of the curriculum structures utilized in BAC Mastery for **3AS Sciences Expérimentales (السنة الثالثة ثانوي — شعبة علوم تجريبية)**.

### Audited Authoritative Sources:
1. **Tier A — Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale (Algérie)**:
   - *Arrêté ministériel n° 54 du 28 juillet 2007* fixant les coefficients des épreuves du Baccalauréat.
   - *Tadarrudjat Sanawiyya (التدرجات السنوية للتعلمات)* — Annual pedagogical progressions published by the General Inspectorate of Pedagogy.
2. **Tier B — Office National des Examens et Concours (ONEC)**:
   - Annales et sujets officiels du Baccalauréat (Sessions 2015–2024).
3. **Tier C — Institut National de Recherche en Éducation (INRE / CNPS)**:
   - Manuels scolaires officiels de 3ème Année Secondaire.

### Audit Classification Scheme:
- `VERIFIED`: Authoritatively confirmed against published ministerial syllabi and official examination criteria.
- `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`: Topic exists in syllabus, but scope or internal sequencing varies across ministerial circulars.
- `UNVERIFIED`: Proposed topic or skill lacking direct ministerial decree confirmation.
- `OUTDATED`: Formerly part of official curriculum; removed by recent educational reforms.
- `MISSING`: Official curriculum unit recognized in national syllabus but not yet modeled in the pilot.
- `DUPLICATE`: Redundant skill overlapping another without distinct assessable value.
