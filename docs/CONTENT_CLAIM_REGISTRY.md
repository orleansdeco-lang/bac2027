## 1. Purpose & Registry Policy

The Content Claim Registry indexes every factual, pedagogical, curriculum, and operational statement made in BAC Mastery. Each claim is explicitly linked to a source from the `CONTENT_SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` and tagged with its rigorous evidentiary classification.

### Classification Categories:
1. **`OFFICIAL_CURRENT`**: Validated by active ministerial pronouncement for BAC 2027.
2. **`OFFICIAL_HISTORICAL`**: Validated by official MEN/ONEC decree from past years; historically authoritative, subject to annual administrative confirmation.
3. **`BAC_MASTERY_DERIVED`**: Proprietary cognitive and educational models created specifically for the BAC Mastery platform.
4. **`RESEARCH_SUPPORTED`**: Grounded in peer-reviewed empirical scientific literature.
5. **`UNVERIFIED`**: Open regulatory questions where explicit current decrees have not yet been promulgated for the 2026-2027 academic session.
